Our general take, in more detail

Don't sell the house to fix this. Selling costs tens of thousands of dollars in fees just to move — that's an expensive way to solve a debt problem you can solve for a fraction of the cost without leaving home. Keep Option 1 on the table only if you want to move for its own sake, not as a way to pay off cards.

Borrowing against your home at a much lower rate (Option 3) is very likely your best move — start with an amount close to your actual card debt, not more. It clears the expensive 26% debt immediately, and the freed-up cash builds your safety cushion far faster than gritting it out at the card's full interest rate (Option 2). Do the two shared cash-flow moves either way — they cost nothing and help every option.

Two numbers on this page are estimates, not facts yet: the rate you'd actually get on a home equity loan, and whether the extra rent is something everyone living with you has actually agreed to. Confirm both before committing to a plan.

  3. 3Switch your mortgage back to a standard monthly payment — it's free money either way.
